The Basics

What Are Photo Magnets?

Photo magnets are custom-made fridge magnets that feature your own photographs. Also called magnetic pictures or magnet photo prints, they combine full-color, high-resolution printing with a flexible magnetic backing, giving you a personal, functional display piece that sticks to any metal surface.

Unlike traditional photo prints that end up in a drawer or a digital file buried in cloud storage, photo magnets for fridge display bring your memories into your everyday environment. Every time you open the refrigerator, you see the people, places, and pets you love most.

"A photo magnet is the cheapest way to turn a digital file into something tangible that you interact with every single day."

Modern magnetic photo prints are produced using inkjet or dye-sublimation technology on flexible magnetic sheets. The best custom magnets for fridge use include a UV-protective laminate that prevents fading and resists moisture, making them durable for years of display on a refrigerator door, filing cabinet, locker, or any steel surface.

Pro Tips

Get the Best Results

  • Use photos with at least 1200 x 1600 pixels for a 4x6 magnet to ensure sharp, crisp printing at 300 DPI.
  • Avoid heavily filtered or low-contrast Instagram edits. Natural colors print more accurately and look better in person.
  • Leave a small margin around the edges of your photo. Some printers trim a millimeter or two on each side during cutting.
  • For group photos, make sure all faces are in focus before ordering. Blurry faces on a large magnet are very noticeable.
  • Order a sample set of 3 to 5 magnets before placing a bulk order to verify color accuracy on your specific printer.
Things to Know

Before You Order

  • Photo magnets do not stick to aluminum or stainless steel non-magnetic fridges. Many modern fridges have non-magnetic doors. Test with a regular fridge magnet first.
  • Direct sunlight will fade any photo magnet over time. Indoor display without sun exposure extends the lifespan significantly.
  • Flexible magnetic sheets have a weaker hold than thick ceramic magnets. They may slide on smooth vertical surfaces if the weight is too heavy.
  • Very dark or very light photos can lose detail in printing. Add slight contrast in your photo editor before uploading for best results.
  • Minimum order quantities apply with some bulk-discount suppliers. Single magnets are available but cost more per unit than sets of 10 or more.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering your first set of custom photo magnets.

What are photo magnets made of?
Photo magnets are made from flexible magnetic sheeting, typically 20 to 30 mil thick. Your photo is printed directly onto the magnetic surface using high-resolution inkjet or dye-sublimation printing, then sealed with a UV-resistant gloss or matte laminate layer. The result is a thin, lightweight magnet that holds well on any ferromagnetic surface.
How long do photo magnets last?
With UV-protective coating and indoor display away from direct sunlight, quality photo magnets last 5 to 10 years without noticeable fading. Magnets left in direct sunlight or humid outdoor environments will fade faster. For maximum longevity, display them on interior surfaces, not on outdoor metal furniture or mailboxes.
Can photo magnets be used on any fridge?
Photo magnets stick to any ferromagnetic steel surface. The majority of refrigerators have magnetic steel doors and work perfectly. However, some premium stainless steel and aluminum-clad fridges are not magnetic. Test with any standard fridge magnet before ordering a large set. If your fridge is not magnetic, a metal magnetic board mounted on a wall is a great alternative display option.
What photo resolution do I need?
For sharp printing at 300 DPI, aim for these minimum file sizes by magnet dimensions: 2x3 inch magnets need at least 600x900 pixels; 3x4 inch magnets need 900x1200 pixels; 4x6 inch magnets need 1200x1800 pixels. Most smartphone photos from 2016 and newer easily meet or exceed these requirements. If your photo looks sharp zoomed in on your phone screen, it will print clearly as a magnet.
Are photo magnets safe for children?
Standard flexible photo magnets are safe and non-toxic. They do not have small detachable parts and use food-safe, water-based inks in most quality production processes. However, very small mini magnets under 1 inch could be a choking hazard for children under three. If ordering magnets that will be within reach of toddlers, choose 3x4 inch or larger sizes to eliminate that risk.
Can I order just one photo magnet?
Yes, most services including ours offer single-magnet orders with no minimum quantity requirement. The per-unit cost is higher for single orders compared to sets of 10 or more, but ordering one first is a great way to check color accuracy and quality before committing to a bulk order for an event or gift set.
What are save the date magnets and how do they work?
Save the date magnets are custom refrigerator magnets printed with an engagement photo and the wedding date, sent to guests 6 to 12 months before the wedding. Unlike paper cards that get lost or thrown away, save the date magnets go straight onto the fridge where guests see them regularly until the big day. They are typically ordered in sets of 50 to 200 for a wedding guest list, and the same design is often reprinted as wedding favor magnets given out at the reception.
Are custom refrigerator magnets the same as fridge magnets?
Yes, custom refrigerator magnets and custom fridge magnets are exactly the same product. The terms are used interchangeably. Whether you search for photo magnets for fridge, personalized fridge magnets, or custom refrigerator magnets, you are looking for the same thing: a flexible magnetic photo print sized to display on a metal refrigerator door.
