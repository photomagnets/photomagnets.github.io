What Magnet Boss covers

The photo magnet business is accessible. Relatively low startup cost, high margin on events, and a product people genuinely love. But there's real complexity in choosing the right equipment, understanding supply chains, and pricing your services correctly. Magnet Boss is being built to document all of it.

Equipment guides

Which printers handle magnet stock well, what cutters to use for clean edges, and how to evaluate UV laminate options without testing every brand yourself.

Supplier directory

Where to source magnetic sheet stock, blank magnet rolls, photo paper, and equipment, with pricing benchmarks so you know what fair looks like.

Pricing strategy

How to price event booths, per-unit consumer orders, and wholesale runs. Margin math, competitive benchmarking, and packaging your offer.

Business playbooks

How to land your first wedding magnet client, what to include in an event proposal, and how to scale from side hustle to full-time operation.
