Why "printed fresh"?

Most custom print companies hold inventory or batch orders together. Fresh Magnets runs a made-to-order model: every magnet is printed specifically for your order, which means better color accuracy, no sitting-in-a-bin degradation, and a print that comes off the press and straight into packaging.

The name is also a nod to the process: fresh off the press, onto your fridge.
