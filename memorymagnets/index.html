The hidden photo problem

The average person takes hundreds of photos a year and looks at almost none of them. They exist on a phone, maybe backed up to a cloud drive, never printed, never displayed. Memory Magnets exists to fix that, turning the photos that matter into something physical and permanent.

A fridge magnet is one of the few objects that earns a permanent spot in daily life. Put a photo there and it becomes part of the household backdrop, seen every morning, pointed out to guests, held onto for years.

What makes a good memory magnet

The right photo

Candid shots, milestone moments, and pet portraits tend to work best. The subject should be clear at small sizes. Tight crops and high contrast land well on a 2×2 magnet.

The right size

2.5×2.5 square is the sweet spot for most portrait photos. Go 2×3 rectangle for landscape shots or wedding save-the-date layouts. Round 2.25" for a playful, less formal feel.

Satin finish

For keepsake magnets meant to last, a satin or matte laminate is more forgiving than gloss, resists fingerprints and handles daily handling well.

Order sets

A single magnet is a gift. A set of 4–6 from a trip, a year, or a family is a whole story. Sets make the fridge into a gallery rather than a spot for one magnet.
